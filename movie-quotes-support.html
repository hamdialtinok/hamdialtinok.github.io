Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to use Movie Quotes?

No. Movie Quotes does not require an account to browse movie quotes or use its core features.

How do I restore my In-App Purchases?

Open the app's Settings screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account used to make the original purchase.

How can I report a bug?

Please email us at hamdialtinok@windowslive.com and include a description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot. We will review your request and assist you.

How can I suggest a movie or TV quote?

Send your suggestion to hamdialtinok@windowslive.com with the movie or TV show title and the quote you would like to suggest.

How can I get help with an In-App Purchase?

If you have a question or problem regarding a Premium subscription or restoring a previous purchase, please contact our support team by email. Include any relevant information or screenshots so that we can assist you.

How can I contact support?

You can contact our support team directly by email. No registration or social media account is required.
