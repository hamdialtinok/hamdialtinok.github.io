Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I restore my In-App Purchases?

Go to the app's Settings screen and tap on the "Restore Purchases" button. Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Do I need to create an account to use the app?

No! You can browse movie quotes and purchase premium features without creating an account.

How can I report a bug or suggest a quote?

Please send an email to hamdialtinok@windowslive.com with details or screenshots, and we will assist you promptly.
